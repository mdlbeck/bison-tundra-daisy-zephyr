🟦 Active & Aorist Passive

Use the 3-1-3 pattern (like πᾶς, πᾶσα, πᾶν).

🟩 All Middle Forms

Use the 2-1-2 pattern (like καλός, καλή, καλόν).

🟧 Perfect Forms

Marked by reduplication. Perfect Mid/Pass endings = Present Mid/Pass.

🔑 2nd Aorist Tip

Look almost identical to Present forms — the stem is the only difference.
